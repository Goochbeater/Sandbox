## 0. How to read this file

Tags on notable items:

- **[CANON]**: Star Wars Legends continuity, confirmed in a search result from a Wookieepedia mirror or an equivalent reference. "Canon" here means Legends canon (the pre-2014 continuity that KOTOR, KOTOR II and The Old Republic belong to). Nothing in this era is current Disney canon.
- **[CANON?]**: Legends continuity from background knowledge or a fan-wiki snippet that did not fully confirm it. Re-check before shipping in-game text.
- **[LOREBOOK]**: from the user's lorebook. The lorebook wins for this game. Conflicts with canon are listed in section 5.
- **[INVENTED]**: created for this project. No source. Safe to use, but never present it as canon.
- **[UNVERIFIED]**: seen once, no reliable source. Do not use until checked.

**Sourcing note.** Wookieepedia could not be fetched directly in this session (the host did not resolve), so facts come from search-result snippets of Wookieepedia mirrors and fan wikis. Sources disagree on several dates; conflicting dates are given as ranges. Treat every [CANON?] item as provisional.

## 2. Factions at 3951 BBY

### 2.1 Galactic Republic

- **State.** The Republic has come through three disasters in a row: the Mandalorian Wars (3963–3960), the Jedi Civil War (3959–3956), and the Purge (3954–3951). It is tired, short of Jedi, and only beginning to reclaim its Sith-held worlds. [CANON?]
- **Military.** Republic Army line infantry, Judicial Forces, and planetary militias. The Judicial Forces flew CR90 corvettes in the late Republic. [CANON?] Standard issue is blaster rifles, blaster carbines, pistols, disruptor rifles, and heavy repeating blasters, with vibro-weapons for close work against Sith. [CANON?]
- **No clone troopers.** The clone army was commissioned around 32 BBY and first fought in the Clone Wars, starting 22 BBY. There are no clone armour plates, no 501st, no commando units, and no DC-15 blasters in 3951. [CANON]
- **No Empire.** The Galactic Empire is not founded until 19 BBY, nearly 3,900 years after this era. The word "Imperial" must not appear in game text. [CANON]
- **Who hunts Rathatri.** Judicial Forces special teams and Republic Army rangers [INVENTED], plus the Order's own hunters (Kyros and Celene). Keep Republic pursuit low-key: exposure is the fail state (design pillar 1).

### 2.2 Jedi Order

- **State.** Gutted by the Triumvirate. Survivors went into exile, and Meetra Surik trained a new generation of what the wiki calls the Lost Jedi. [CANON?] Sources disagree on whether a formal High Council sits in 3951. Use "a council of survivors" for in-game safety. [INVENTED]
- **Ranks.** Initiate or Youngling (child in Temple basic training). Padawan (after the Initiate Trials, chosen by a Knight or Master). Knight (passed the Jedi Trials, works alone, may take a Padawan). Master (trained a Knight or showed exceptional wisdom; only Masters sit on the High Council). [CANON?]
- **Battlemaster.** This is a **teaching title, not a rank**. It belongs to the Order's top lightsaber instructor, chosen by the High Council. After the Ruusan Reformations there was normally only one at a time. Kao Cen Darach and the Hero of Tython held it. [CANON?] The lorebook's "Battlemaster" Aiden Kyros works as "the Order's last combat master," but the wording should read as the academy's chief instructor, not a field rank. [LOREBOOK] (minor conflict)
- **Knight classes.** Game-era Knights sorted into specialisms (Sentinel, Guardian, Consular). [CANON?] Use only as flavour.

### 2.3 Sith remnants

- The Sith Empire is gone by 3950. [CANON?] Remnant cults and relic hunters remain.
- **Sion's assassins.** Sion sent Sith assassins against the Jedi during the Purge. [CANON?] Masterless ones make good mid-tier enemies and recruitment fodder. [INVENTED]
- **Nadd's cult.** Defeated in 3998 BBY, so its worship is ancient. [CANON?] Rathatri's "dark nexus" on Dxun can be framed as a leftover of this cult. [INVENTED]
- **Rathatri as Sith heir.** No canon heir exists. The lorebook's apprentice-turned-Sith is [LOREBOOK] and [INVENTED] in full.

### 2.4 The Exchange, the Hutts, and Hutta

- **Hutta** is the Hutt homeworld. [CANON] The lorebook's slave pits and cantina arena are [LOREBOOK].
- **The Hutt Cartel** is a business alliance of Hutt clans that is also a crime syndicate with internal feuds. [CANON?]
- **The Exchange** is a criminal network from later Old Republic material. [CANON?] For 3951, treat it as loose smuggling cells and forgers. [INVENTED]

### 2.5 Czerka

- Czerka Corporation is a large mining and manufacturing concern with sites across the Outer Rim. [CANON]
- Czerka's HK-24 design is the base blueprint for HK-47. [CANON?]
- A Czerka expedition on Tatooine failed during the Civil War. [CANON?]
- **Game use.** Czerka is the deniable supplier for Rathatri's junkyard shipyards and the buyer of salvaged Sith tech. [INVENTED]

### 2.6 Mandalorian remnants

- Dxun was the Mandalorians' Inner Rim base in the war, and the battle over it was one of the bloodiest. [CANON?]
- After the war a Mandalorian group camped on Dxun to rebuild their order. How long it lasted is unknown. [CANON?]
- The Mandalore "the Indomitable" died on Dxun. [CANON?] Keep the Indomitable and Mandalore the Ultimate as separate figures. [CANON?]
- **Set dressing.** Mandalorian war-camp ruins on Dxun. [INVENTED]

### 2.7 Bounty hunters

- No guild is needed; contracts are offered and taken by independents. [INVENTED]
- **Do not reference Boba Fett.** He is Jango Fett's clone and belongs to the clone-era. [CANON?] The design doc's "Boba Fett tech" and "SLV" references are design-level only and must not appear as in-world names.

### 2.8 Trandoshans

- **Scorekeeper.** The Trandoshan goddess who rewards combat and hunting success. [CANON?] Kills earn "Jagganath points." [CANON?]
- **Life debt.** Lorebook only. [LOREBOOK] Unverified as Legends canon. Keep, but treat as game fiction.
- **Do not use the CIS-mercenary origin** that one fan wiki gives for a Trandoshan slaving group. That origin is clone-era. [UNVERIFIED]

## 3. The Force, the Sith, and the tech

### 3.1 "Darth" before Bane

- "Darth" predates Darth Bane's Rule of Two. Early holders include Darth Atrius, Darth Caldoth and Darth Noctyss. [CANON?]
- Bane's Rule of Two later kept the title to one master and one apprentice at a time. [CANON?]
- The word is generally glossed as "Dark Lord." [CANON]
- In 3951 it is a self-claim, and the galaxy only recognises it once the holder has the power to back it. This matches the lorebook's Traya scene. [LOREBOOK]
- Kreia used "Darth Traya" as a mask rather than a true identity. [CANON?] Matches her lorebook line "Darth Traya was the mask I wore." [LOREBOOK]

### 3.2 Sith philosophy

- The Sith of this era value ambition, power, and survival. Sith texts Kreia read claimed the Force betrays Force-users and that they live in a broken universe. [CANON?]
- **Game framing.** The Sith are a path rather than a rulebook. Rathatri's "dark side" is survival-driven, not doctrine-driven. [INVENTED]

### 3.3 Kreia / Traya: canon versus lorebook

**Canon (Legends).** Kreia hates the Force itself. She wants it destroyed. She rejects both the light/dark divide and the Force's predestination. [CANON?] The Malachor V battle cut Surik's connection to the Force. Kreia planned to use the Exile's bonding ability to create an "echo," a wound in the Force that would spread across the galaxy. [CANON?] "Force-wound" and "the Force is a wound" are the right in-world phrasing.

**Lorebook.** Manipulation, seeing "cracks" in a ship or a mind, fear as a baseline, the illusion of choice, strength through suffering. [LOREBOOK] These are not Kreia's canon teachings. Keep them, but label them as her lorebook voice.

**Recommended synthesis.** The holocron is a recording made before Malachor V. Traya lectures on the Force as a wound and on the cracks in people (lorebook), while her arc argues against the Force itself (canon). This also explains why her lessons feel cruel without being simple villainy. [INVENTED]

### 3.4 HK-47

- Built by Revan after the Mandalorian Wars from a Czerka HK-24 blueprint, with Revan's modifications. [CANON?]
- The "meatbag" insult originated with HK-47's grudge against Revan's apprentice. [CANON?]
- **Design rule.** Do not put the named canon HK-47 into the game. Use "HK-class assassin databank" or "Czerka-pattern assassin droid" for the Pattern-Breaker databank in design §6 3.3. [INVENTED]

### 3.5 Force-masking and Sith assassins

- **Force-masking.** Kreia's camouflage, distraction, and "dead zones" come from the lorebook. [LOREBOOK] Naturally occurring Force voids called dead zones are [INVENTED]. The "echo," a lasting absence left in the Force, comes from Kreia's canon plan. [CANON?]
- **Sith assassins.** Sion used them against the Jedi. [CANON?] That gives Rathatri's assassins a real precedent in the era.

### 3.6 Sith alchemy, holocrons, kyber, tainted and synthetic crystals

- **Sith alchemy.** Sith hardening and enchanting of blades and hilts. [CANON?] Use for alchemised hilts and assassin robes.
- **Holocrons.** Store teachings and can be locked against the unworthy. [CANON?] A Sith holocron of Nadd was found and studied by Darth Bane in 1000 BBY. [CANON?]
- **Kyber.** Lightsabers, Jedi and Sith alike, are built around kyber crystals. [CANON] A Sith can corrupt a kyber crystal, which turns it red. [CANON?] "Tainted kyber" is the in-game name for these. [CANON?]
- **Synthetic crystals.** Red synthetic crystals have been in use since the Old Sith Empire. [UNVERIFIED] Treat as a Sith workaround that avoids the corruption step. [UNVERIFIED]
- **Bone hilts.** The lorebook's bone-handled saber. [LOREBOOK] No source found. [INVENTED] Keep as flavour, and tie it to the kill that made it.

## 5. Factual corrections and lorebook conflicts

**Design doc §2 errors to fix:**

- "Triumvirate destroyed by Meetra Surik in 3951 BBY" is broadly correct. The detail is wrong: Nihilus died at Telos IV, not at Malachor V. Sion and Traya/Kreia fell at Malachor V (Trayus Academy), and Kreia's fate conflicts across sources. [CANON?]
- "Dxun ... served as Nihilus's Sith staging base in 3951 BBY." **Not supported.** Nihilus worked from his fleet and attacked worlds from space. The Triumvirate base was Trayus Academy on Malachor V. Recommend moving the Nihilus reference or dropping it. [INVENTED to replace]

**Lorebook conflicts:**

- **Raxus Prime** (see anachronism 5). The lorebook wins for gameplay but must be revised to remove the CIS.
- **Ambria.** Lorebook has "two fallen Jedi turned Sith rivals." Legends has a Sith sorceress who built an obelisk and destroyed much of the world's life. [CANON?] Keep the lorebook version and borrow the obelisk as a set piece. [LOREBOOK + INVENTED]
- **Lenico IV.** Lorebook: abandoned, strange energy readings. Legends: Exal Kressh's massacre (c. 3678 BBY). [CANON?] Use the massacre as the cause of the energy readings. [INVENTED link]
- **Krayiss II.** No canon found. [INVENTED] Treat as a game-only site.
- **Milian, Zx34, Aiden Kyros, Celene Tryst, Eren Rythus, Vaara, Bosskarr, The Harbinger, Nightbringer, The Shroud.** No canon found for any of these. [INVENTED] Do not claim them as Legends.
