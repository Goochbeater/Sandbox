# DARK LEGACY: RATHATRI - Cast Bible

Who speaks, how they sound, what they want, and how to tell them apart at low-poly distance. Every character is an adult. Dialogue lines are in `05_script.md` and `script.json`; speaker IDs below match those files.

Timeline used for ages: Act I opens 3951 BBY. Milian dies in 1.7 (3951). Act V is about 3945 BBY.

## Main cast

### Rathatri (`RATHATRI`), the hero

- **Role:** Born into the Hutta pens, Slave 426. Taken into the pits at 19. Milian's apprentice for about six years. Exiled on Dxun from 3951. Becomes the hidden Sith rumor at the centre of the game.
- **Age:** 19 at pit entry, 26 at Milian's death, early 30s in Act V.
- **Visual, by act:**
  - *Stage I (Act I):* thin and wiry, ash-grey-brown rags, scar overlay on the forearms and face (the scar mask is a shader, not geometry), no weapon but a shiv.
  - *Stage III (Acts I-III):* black combat leathers, a bone-white hilt with a crimson blade, waist cloth panels.
  - *Stage V (Acts IV-V):* charcoal-black coat with a flared cloak, a crimson emissive Sith sigil on the right sleeve, the kyber vent glowing through the bone grip.
- **Silhouette:** Narrow shoulders in Stage I, a wide dark cape in Stage V. The cloak is the signature silhouette.
- **Signature prop:** The bone-hilt crimson saber (hilt from a beast he killed on Hutta).
- **Voice:** Short sentences, few words. He answers questions with threats or with a question back. Early on he curses and bluffs. By Act V he speaks with Traya's cold vocabulary but keeps his pit habits (he never says please).
- **Sample lines:**
  - "Keep your Force. I'll take the power and leave the priests."
  - "Let them bet on me. I'll collect every credit."
  - "Teach me, and I'll outlive you. That's the whole deal."
- **Wants:** Power strong enough that no master, Jedi, or Hutt can own him again. Revenge on the Jedi who killed Milian. Secrets that Milian kept from him. Under the surface: he wants to be the one who decides what happens to him.
- **Arc:** From a slave who wants to live, to a lord who wants to be feared, to a man who has to decide whether the title is worth what it costs his people.

### Sith Witch Milian (`MILIAN`)

- **Role:** Rathatri's master. Finds him in the arena, takes him from Hutta, and trains him on Zx34. Dies in 1.7 to Kyros and Celene.
- **Age:** 60s. Human (the lorebook does not give a species).
- **Visual:** Long steel-grey hair (`Hair_Long`, grey `0x9aa0a8`), a weathered face with wrinkle shading, dark witch robes with plum-black base and muted violet trim (`0x1a1220` / `0x403050`), a hooded cloak, bronze Sith emblems. No lightsaber.
- **Silhouette:** Tall, still, and narrow. She never moves quickly, so her hood and hem carry her motion.
- **Signature prop:** A pale gold holocron held in one hand and a low-poly ritual dagger with emissive runes on the belt.
- **Voice:** Precise, cool, and never loud. Aphorisms. She talks down to people as a matter of habit. She calls Rathatri "Four-Two-Six" until he earns his name (1.5), and then "Rathatri" only.
- **Sample lines:**
  - "Pain is a door. Walk through it, or stay on the hinges."
  - "Dead Sith have more to teach than living Jedi. Study the scrolls."
  - "I make no promises of freedom. Only a different leash."
- **Wants:** To keep her apprentice as a weapon, and to keep her own secrets. She wants power and mastery, and she is afraid of being replaced.
- **Lorebook line (verbatim, 1.5):** "You are persistent. Like a parasite that refuses to die. That caught my interest."

### Aiden Kyros (`KYROS`), Jedi Battlemaster

- **Role:** Hunts Sith for two decades. Kills Milian in 1.7. Boss of the Act V siege (5.2). Dies in the Nadd crypt.
- **Age:** Late fifties. Human, grey-buzzed hair, a face of old scars.
- **Visual:** Ranger armor in tan and steel, a long tan coat with a steel gorget, matched worn-steel dual hilts with wrapped grips and two blue blades. No hood, so his face shows. Broad shoulders (rmale x1.06).
- **Silhouette:** Square and upright. Two blades held low in a Battlemaster stance.
- **Signature prop:** The paired blue sabers. The hilts are a matched set and the wrapped grips show wear.
- **Voice:** Low, measured, formal, and almost never raised. Few contractions. He calls Rathatri "Sith" the way other people spit. He is not cruel, just certain.
- **Sample lines:**
  - "I have buried better Sith than you. Each one swore he was different."
  - "Peace is not the absence of struggle, but mastery of it."
  - "Listen before you strike. The enemy always whispers first."
- **Wants:** To prevent another war like the Mandalorian Wars or the Jedi Civil War. He wants the Sith gone, and he wants the Jedi to rebuild without fear. He is haunted by Milian.

### Celene Tryst (`CELENE`), Padawan, later Jedi Knight

- **Role:** Kyros's apprentice. She is at Milian's death in 1.7. Her duel with Rathatri is 3.4 (a boss fight with a scripted near-defeat). She arrives broken in 5.3 and is the choice that decides the ending.
- **Age:** 23 at 1.7, Padawan. About 29 by Act V, a Jedi Knight in grey.
- **Visual:** Auburn hair in two buns. Tan Padawan robes with braided cords in Act III, knight greys in Act V. A double-bladed saberstaff, green blade turning green-white by Act V.
- **Silhouette:** Quick and lean, with the saberstaff held in the middle so the blade reaches both ways.
- **Signature prop:** The green saberstaff with a wrapped slender hilt.
- **Voice:** Earnest, quick, and sometimes sarcastic. She teases when she is confident and goes flat and clipped when she is frightened. After Kyros dies she sounds like someone who has stopped expecting an answer.
- **Sample lines:**
  - "Kyros said you'd run. I said you'd crawl first."
  - "There's no honor in a trap."
  - "I want nothing you can give me, Sith."
- **Wants:** To prove herself worthy of her master. Later, to understand what she is and why her Order failed the enclave. She does not want to kill, and she does not want to kneel.

### Darth Traya (`TRAYA`), holocron mentor

- **Role:** Lives inside a cracked black-gold holocron on the Dxun command table. The mentor who judges Rathatri at 2.5, 3.1, 3.4, 4.1-4.3, and 5.4. She is the spine of the game's upgrade system.
- **Form:** No body. A hooded hologram in cold blue light, with a face half lost in shadow. One hand is visible, with a carved ring that catches the light.
- **Visual:** Pale, hooded silhouette in the HOLO material from the Order 66 kit. The holocron itself is cracked with a cold blue glow, and the blue light gets brighter when she is angry. Not a shape the player should read as a person, so the face stays in shadow.
- **Voice:** Dry, aphoristic, cruel, and quietly amused. She asks questions she already has answers to. She dismisses both the Jedi and the Sith as "your masters." She speaks as if every lesson is a test that Rathatri will fail. She never explains a lesson fully.
- **Sample lines (the first two are new, in her register; the third is verbatim from the lorebook):**
  - "Teaching is not what I do. I sharpen. There is a difference."
  - "A shroud is a kindness only to the one who hides beneath it."
  - "Your victories hinge on your victims' mistakes, not your mastery."
- **Wants:** To use Rathatri as a mirror for her own fall. She says she wants him to become a weapon with a mind of its own, and she is curious how far he will go.
- **Lorebook lines (verbatim):** the judgments in 2.5, 3.1, 3.4, and 5.4 use the quoted lines from the lorebook without changes. Her other lines are written in her register.

### Bosskarr (`BOSSKARR`), Trandoshan slaver captain

- **Role:** Captain of the freighter *Jagganath's Fang*. He leads a slaver party that Rathatri ambushes at 2.2. If Rathatri spares him, he swears a life-debt. He commands the other two ships in the fleet, *Skullrender* and *Stalker's Shadow*.
- **Age:** Mid-life veteran. Trandoshans are long-lived, so "veteran" reads as scarred, not old.
- **Visual:** Scaled grey-green head with a scar across the snout, olive flight suit in the CLONE-family grade, clawed hands, a customized hunting rifle (CAST.dc15 retinted). He wears a trophy belt. Rathatri took his ceremonial mask after the ambush, and it hangs in Rathatri's quarters.
- **Silhouette:** Heavy and low, with a long snout and a rifle held across the body.
- **Signature prop:** The hunting rifle with a bone-pale stock.
- **Voice:** Trandoshan speech in Basic. Short, heavy sentences. He drops articles when he is excited and uses hunt words ("prey," "tally," "debt," "road"). He says "Scorekeeper" as a ritual phrase. He grows more formal when he owes someone.
- **Scorekeeper background:** Trandoshans in canon honor a goddess who keeps score of kills and hunts. The dead are judged by what they tallied, and a broken oath is a mark on the ledger. The life-debt in 2.2a is built on that belief.
- **Sample lines:**
  - "Scorekeeper watches. Who walks into my hunt-road?"
  - "Life spared. Life owed. The Scorekeeper writes it in the ledger."
  - "Trandoshan ledger never lies. The Scorekeeper forgets nothing, not even your face."
- **Wants:** Profit, respect, and survival. He wants a patron who wins, so he can stay in the game. Under that: he wants his debts paid and his tally kept.

### Vaara (`VAARA`), head assassin

- **Role:** Rathatri's most trusted assassin. Double-bladed crimson saber, sleeve vibro-daggers, and poisoned blades for finishers.
- **Age:** 35. Human. Born 3980 BBY, sold to Trandoshans at 16, freed by Rathatri at 26 (3954 BBY, see continuity notes).
- **Visual:** Long, dark brown wavy hair. Dark green eyes and tan skin. Athletic build. Layered dark wraps with red thread stitching. Retractable vibro-daggers at the wrists. The crimson double-blade hangs from the belt.
- **Silhouette:** Long hair and loose wraps that trail when she moves. Her silhouette is the tallest of the assassins.
- **Signature prop:** The crimson double-bladed saber with the sleeve daggers.
- **Voice:** Clipped, sardonic, and flat. She says less than she means. She answers Rathatri without flattery and gives short, practical orders in combat.
- **Sample lines:**
  - "I am not afraid of anything. I have already lost everything." (lorebook)
  - "I will do whatever it takes to protect Rathatri." (lorebook)
  - "Give me a target. I'd rather bleed for you than sit in this temple."
- **Wants:** To belong somewhere. To make her past pay. Her loyalty is real, and it is bound up with the idea that Rathatri is the only one who understands her.
- **Lorebook quotes:** Her quotes come verbatim from the lorebook.

### Eren Rythus (`EREN`), head assassin

- **Role:** Second head assassin. Burned in a childhood skirmish, held in slavery, freed by Rathatri. His armor is Sith-alchemy work. He is the squad's tactician.
- **Age:** 35, 6'0" (183 cm), muscular. Human. Born 3980 BBY, home attacked at 10 (3970 BBY), sold at 16, freed at 26 (3954 BBY).
- **Visual:** Short black hair, brown eyes. Burn scar shader down the right side of the head and face. The right arm is gone below the elbow and replaced with a rigid black-metal prosthetic forearm on the shared rig. Armored tunic. A compact energy buckler on the left arm. The crimson double-bladed staff.
- **Silhouette:** Broad and square. The buckler and the black forearm are the two readable shapes.
- **Signature prop:** The black-metal prosthetic forearm with the buckler.
- **Voice:** Measured, dry, deliberate. He talks like a farmer who has become a soldier: crops, fields, fences, and ash. He is quieter than the rest of the squad and says the hardest things calmly.
- **Sample lines (new; the lorebook gives Eren Vaara's quotes, so these replace them):**
  - "Burned fields grow back, if you hide the fire."
  - "A man who leans gets knocked over. Milian leaned."
  - "Trust the one who never needs to be asked twice."
- **Wants:** Revenge on the Jedi who burned his village. Meaning for the years he lost. He wants his suffering to have a purpose, and Rathatri gives him one.

### The Elite Five (`IRRIN`, `SSARRA`, `DORN`, `PELL`, `MESK`)

Five assassins with pikes. Each wears a partial mask in a different tint so they can be told apart at a distance. Shared kit: dark tunic and pants, a Force pike with a shock tip (instanced x5), a stealth generator that flickers when it is strained. Base colour is dark red (`0.6, 0.1, 0.1`). Per-person detail below.

| ID | Name | Species, age | Mask tint | Signature trait |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `IRRIN` | Irrin Vask | Human, 27 | Bone-white band | Hums while oiling her pike |
| `SSARRA` | Ssarra Vel | Twi'lek, 31 | Green lens | Keeps a ledger of debts |
| `DORN` | Dorn Osk | Zabrak, 29 | Dark-red plate | Mutters his kill count |
| `PELL` | Pell Ossa | Sullustan, 34 | Grey cloth | Pockets shiny things and returns them |
| `MESK` | Mesk Tarrin | Rodian, 25 | Yellow goggles over the mask | Names pikes after lost targets |

**Irrin Vask** (Human, 27)
- *Backstory:* A Force-sensitive who hid her talent working debt on a mining moon. A Trandoshan net caught her and Rathatri's people bought her out.
- *Quirk:* Hums lullabies while she oils her pike, and stops when anyone looks at her.
- *Loyalty trigger:* Rathatri says the name of her younger sister, who died in the mine. He has never met her, and he remembers the name anyway.
- *Voice:* Warm, then suddenly clipped.

**Ssarra Vel** (Twi'lek, 31)
- *Backstory:* Former accountant for a Hutt-court smuggling ring. She skimmed one credit too many and had to disappear.
- *Quirk:* Keeps a ledger of every debt owed to her, in her head and on paper. She never forgives an unpaid debt, and she always says so.
- *Loyalty trigger:* Rathatri pays one of her debts in full, with interest, in front of witnesses.
- *Voice:* Precise, numerical, and dry.

**Dorn Osk** (Zabrak, 29)
- *Backstory:* A Republic militia conscript who killed his own sergeant in a bad raid. He deserted and is now hunted by the same units the commandos come from.
- *Quirk:* Never takes his mask off in company. He mutters his kill count under his breath.
- *Loyalty trigger:* Rathatri takes the blame for a failed mission in front of Bosskarr's crews, instead of letting Dorn carry it.
- *Voice:* Gruff and few words, with a sudden flash of humor.

**Pell Ossa** (Sullustan, 34)
- *Backstory:* A Fringe cartographer who mapped Bosskarr's slave routes. Rathatri bought his silence and kept him alive.
- *Quirk:* Pockets shiny things and gives them back with an apology. He loves maps and draws them constantly.
- *Loyalty trigger:* Rathatri lets him keep his surveys, and gives him a map of the Dxun temple in return.
- *Voice:* Nervous and polite, with excellent memory.

**Mesk Tarrin** (Rodian, 25)
- *Backstory:* A Coruscant gutter-runner who, as an adult, taught herself to kill from stolen holovids and a salvaged vibroknife. She has never met a Jedi.
- *Quirk:* Names every pike she sharpens after a target she lost. She wears yellow lenses over the mask.
- *Loyalty trigger:* Rathatri lets her choose the target and backs her choice, even when it is wrong.
- *Voice:* Cheerful and reckless, with a magpie's curiosity and a killer's flatness.

### Pit-master Tohgrun Vak (`VAK`), Hutt pit-master

- **Role:** Runs the Hutta pit and works for Hutt house Gorvanth. The announcer and bookmaker of the arena in Act I.
- **Species:** Vurrin (invented): squat, hairless, a bulbous skull, and gill slits along the throat that spray a bitter oil when he is angry.
- **Visual:** Hairless bulb-headed silhouette. Brass-banded gaff in one hand, a betting ledger in the other, a stained apron.
- **Voice:** Loud and theatrical. A ringmaster's patter. He talks to the crowd more than to the fighters.
- **Sample lines:**
  - "Bets are open! Four-Two-Six fights for the Hutt's pleasure!"
  - "Someone with a voice like a grave and a fat purse. Fight!"
  - "What in the Hutt's name was that?"
- **Wants:** Favor with his Hutt master and the house's money. He would sell a fighter's contract for a good wager.

### Interceptor officer, Lieutenant Mirek Donn (`DONN`)

- **Role:** The young pilot of the Sith Interceptor *Umbral Wake* in 3.2. He is overwhelmed and promoted two weeks before the fight. He is Sith Empire remnant, one of the few still flying.
- **Age:** 21. Human.
- **Visual:** Crimson-trimmed flight suit with new lieutenant pips. Cracked visor. The Interceptor itself is a dagger-shaped hull.
- **Voice:** Young, clipped, and panicky, then pleading. Lines break up when he is afraid.
- **Sample lines:**
  - "Lieutenant Donn, Umbral Wake. Mayday! Stabilizers aren't holding!"
  - "It's pulling me! I have the stick, I have the... no. No!"
  - "I was promoted two weeks ago. Please, I'm twenty-one..."
- **Wants:** To survive, and to be someone who matters. He wants his promotion to mean something.

### Jedi seeker, Halun Tesk (`TESK`)

- **Role:** A Miraluka seeker of the rebuilt Order. He senses artifact pulses and converges on dig sites. He is also the name the Wellspring of Doubt takes in 5.3.
- **Age:** 26. Miraluka (no eyes; he sees the Force directly).
- **Visual:** Pale grey hooded CLOAK with a wrapped cloth across the eye sockets. A single green blade. The hood's shape is the silhouette, and the blindfold reads as a face from a distance.
- **Signature prop:** The green blade held low, with the wrapped face cloth.
- **Voice:** Gentle, precise, and calm. He describes the Force as shape and colour, because he sees it that way: "a wound of light," "a shadow with edges."
- **Sample lines:**
  - "A pulse echoes under the stone. Listen."
  - "Your fear reveals your path, Sith."
  - "I sense the mask, and the mask senses me."
- **Wants:** To find the dark pulses and end them. He keeps the enclave's secret. He is fragile, and the Wellspring breaks him.

### Republic commando sergeant, Ossian Keel (`KEEL`)

- **Role:** The sergeant of a militia commando fireteam in Iziz (4.4). His shield wall is the first boss encounter with commandos. He drops the power cell when he dies (design doc `drop: 'cell'`).
- **Age:** 44. Human.
- **Visual:** Olive-grey plastoid armor, a visored helmet, and an orange pauldron (`0xb06020`) that marks him as sergeant. A shield wall of rigid panels.
- **Silhouette:** Square and armored. The orange pauldron is the readable mark.
- **Signature prop:** The shield-wall panel and the orange pauldron.
- **Voice:** Flat, professional, dry. "Copy." "Hold the line." He uses humor only as a weapon.
- **Sample lines:**
  - "Target is a Sith. Repeat, Sith. Form the shield wall."
  - "Hope's got armor, Sith. Your mouth doesn't."
  - "Tell the Republic... the Sith came through the wall."
- **Wants:** To bring the rumor home alive, and to turn a Sith into paperwork.

### Bounty hunter, Dagan Roe (`ROE`)

- **Role:** Leads the bounty hunter pair in 2.6. Freelance, paid by the Hutts and by anyone with credits. He is the chattiest enemy in the game.
- **Species and age:** Duros, 38. Blue-skinned, with a large head and large eyes.
- **Visual:** Mismatched armor plates in orange (`1, 0.7, 0.2`), a jetpack, a whip-cord reel on the hip, and a helmet with a gold visor. He never dresses the same way twice.
- **Silhouette:** The jetpack and the reel make a wide, loose shape. The orange is the colour that reads.
- **Signature prop:** The whip-cord reel on the hip.
- **Voice:** Chatty mercenary business talk. He makes a joke of every fight and shows his price like a salesman.
- **Sample lines:**
  - "Bounty's on a rumor. Pays well. Dead or breathing, same fee."
  - "Smells like a Sith, sounds like a cave. Let's go, partner."
  - "Price just went up, Sith. Dead or breathing."
- **Wants:** Credits. He'd happily sell the bounty and take the fee. He wants his fee paid in full.

## Extra voices (non-body or minor)

- **Freedon Nadd (`NADD`), spirit guardian (4.5).** The Sith lord of the iron tomb, speaking from an obsidian throne. Archaic and grand. He is contemptuous of Rathatri's "wisdom" and tests him before he gives the shard. Visual: a faint blue-black figure in tomb light. Lines: "Who disturbs the iron? Who stirs the dead?"
- **Mirror (`MIRROR`), Lenico illusion (4.2).** A false Rathatri that knows every flinch. Visual: the same silhouette as Rathatri, but in clean leather and without the scars. Voice: his own voice, slowed. Line: "Then what are you, Rathatri? A pit slave with a borrowed blade."
- **Databank DB-9 "Tallow" (`DB`), Raxus Prime (3.3).** A surviving combat droid databank. Flat, literal, and murderously practical. Written in the cadence of the databank droids in canon, with lines built around "Statement:", "Query:", and "Analysis:". Visual: a broken battle-droid head on a cable spool, with a single amber sensor.
- **Holocron echo (`HOLOCRON`), Dxun tomb roots (2.4).** A dead apprentice trapped in the corrupted holocron. Whispery and fragmented. Visual: a dim flicker of rune light in the dirt.

## Continuity notes and decisions

These are decisions made while writing. Each one should be reviewed against the lorebook and design doc before the game ships.

1. **Vaara and Eren, "rescued at 26."** The lorebook frees both of them at 26. With both at 35 in Act V, that puts the rescue at 3954 BBY. That is during Milian's apprenticeship, before the game opens, so Rathatri frees them as Milian's apprentice and they serve him for nine years. Decision: rescue at 3954 BBY.
2. **Eren and the Force.** The lorebook says he is not Force-sensitive but has a dark-side connection, telekinesis, and telepathy, and also wields a lightsaber. Decision: he has no Force powers. His strength and endurance come from the Sith-alchemy armor. The crimson double-bladed saber follows the design doc, which is the newer source.
3. **Eren's childhood.** The lorebook says he was 10 when his home was destroyed and also calls him "a young man at the time." Decision: he was 10. The "young man" line is treated as an error.
4. **Eren's quotes.** The lorebook gives Eren the same four quotes as Vaara. The new Eren lines in this file replace them. Vaara keeps the lorebook quotes.
5. **Traya's judgments and the duel.** The lorebook puts the "caged beast" judgment after the Celene duel. The design doc places it at 2.5, before the duel. Decision: "caged beast" is at 2.5 as the design doc requires, verbatim. "Your victories hinge on your victims' mistakes" is at 3.4 after the duel, as the lorebook has it.
6. **The ending map.** The design doc gives Celene's choice (5.3) and the title choice (5.4) but does not define how they combine. Decision: a 2x2 map. Execute + Claim = The Reaper of the Rebuild. Corrupt + Claim = True, Darth Rathatri. Execute + Purge = Alone in the Warrens. Corrupt + Purge = The Corrupter. This makes the "true ending" depend on corrupting Celene, not only on keeping the assassins. Confirm before the endings are built.
7. **Bosskarr's name.** It is close to Bossk, the canon Trandoshan bounty hunter. Consider renaming before a public build.
8. **Trandoshan point term.** The lorebook spells the ship *Jagganath's Fang*. The Trandoshan scoring term in canon is "Jagannath points." Kept as spelled in the lorebook.
9. **Traya's voice.** The lorebook quotes are used verbatim. All other Traya lines are original in her register. No Kreia lines are reused.
10. **Milian's death year.** The design doc caption and this script use 3951 BBY. `01_era_and_factions.md` puts it at c. 3953 BBY. The Vaara and Eren rescue (3954 BBY) still falls inside Milian's apprenticeship under either date, so the 3951 caption can be changed without touching the cast ages.
11. **"Battlemaster."** `01_era_and_factions.md` treats it as a teaching title held by one instructor, not a field rank. Kyros keeps the lorebook's usage. Confirm the wording before the title card is shown.
