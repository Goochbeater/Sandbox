## 0. How to read this file

Each fact carries one tag:

- **[L]** Lorebook or master build. This is the game's own continuity.
- **[S]** Sourced in research. These are Legends-era fan-wiki or walkthrough summaries reached through search. Verify before final in-game text (see section 8).
- **[D]** Design proposal. Invented for the game so it plays well. Not canon.

Almost all [S] material is Star Wars Legends. The game is Legends-framed, so that is acceptable, but check each claim before it ships.

**Sourcing limits.** Wookieepedia (starwars.fandom.com) and the Archivum mirror both returned a proxy policy denial (HTTP 403) when fetched directly, and the walkthrough domains did not resolve. I did not route around the denial. The facts below come from search-result summaries of those pages. Several conflict with each other, and the conflicts are called out.

## 2. Dxun

### 2.1 Orbit, status and scale

- Dxun is the largest of the four moons of Onderon [S]. It is covered almost entirely by dense jungle full of fierce predators [S].
- A Legends claim says Dxun and Onderon's atmospheres touched once a year, which let drexl fly across [S, low confidence]. Use it as a rumour the Beast Riders tell.
- Orbital period, gravity and day length are not sourced. [D] Use 0.9 g and a 26-hour day.

### 2.2 Climate and weather

- Heavy foliage and frequent storms [S].
- [D] Constant humidity. Rain bursts every 6 to 8 game-minutes in a storm cycle. Valley fog in the mornings. A storm front turns the sky grey-green and cuts visibility to about 40 m.

### 2.3 Jungle layers

Layer structure is [D], grounded in [S] notes on canopy hunters, cave-dwelling parasites and predatory plants.

1. **Canopy (30 to 40 m).** Drexl roosts and maalraa lookouts. Light comes down in shafts.
2. **Understorey (5 to 25 m).** Ferns, vines and cannok runs.
3. **Forest floor.** Mud, root-arches and fog. Orbalisk caves open in dark rock outcrops [S].
4. **Ruin layer.** Stone overgrown with moss, Mandalorian bunkers and Sith ruins. Predatory flora thrives here [S].

### 2.4 The Tomb of Freedon Nadd

**History [S].** Nadd's first tomb was a cave beneath the royal palace in Iziz. After his worshippers were defeated in 3998 BBY, Jedi Master Arca Jeth had a new tomb built on Dxun. It held Nadd, Queen Amanoa and King Ommin, the last of the dynasty.

**Exterior [S].** A black and dark-grey spire on a hill above the valley. It is built of Mandalorian iron, is the only artificial structure on the moon, and has a broad landing pad at its base. Sources report that no vegetation has grown on it in about four thousand years. Exar Kun cut into the outer wall about a year after sealing, per one fan-wiki summary. Other accounts give a different date.

**Interior [S and D].** Darth Bane found Nadd's holocron and orbalisks inside. KOTOR II's version is a sequence of rooms (section 2.6). No source found gives the full arrangement.

**Architecture [D].** Treat it as a stepped trapezoid: a wide base with a sloping apron, three stacked tiers each set back, and a narrowing spire. The trench and spiked bastion from the master build can be a [D] addition: a dry moat ringed by iron cones.

### 2.5 Mandalorian ruins and the war

- The Mandalorians used Dxun as a fleet command post [S]. A moon base under Mandalore the Ultimate launched operations against the Republic [S].
- A Neo-Crusader headquarters on Dxun is described in one listing [S, low confidence].
- Defences: hidden minefields, traps, anti-air turrets and jungle wildlife [S]. Craters, wrecked hardware and bodies litter the jungle floor [S].
- Revan's assault used hundreds of small-unit feints [S]. Meetra Surik's unit lost most of its soldiers crossing a minefield [S].
- The Mandalorians claimed a ten-to-one kill ratio. This is a Mandalorian claim, not a fact [S].
- KOTOR II's Mandalorian ruins include a numbered bunker (25A) and a dead Mandalorian on the jungle path [S].

### 2.6 KOTOR II's Dxun layout (as sourced)

1. **Jungle Landing (Ebon Hawk area).** The landing zone. A shuttle wreck with a rusted computer port sits along the jungle path, and the port can be repaired to power sentry droids [S].
2. **Jungle #3, Mandalorian camp.** The party is escorted to the Mandalorian leader whether or not they resist [S].
3. **Mandalorian Ruins.** A Guard Captain offers the zakkeg hunt. The beast is at Jungle #9, and the proof is its ear [S].
4. **Jungle Tomb.** Mines and perimeter sensors. Bao-Dur supplies the codes [S].
5. **Sith Tomb.** The entrance is held by four Sith and a droid. The ritual room (#7) is locked and opens from #5 or #6. A Sith Master and two Dark Jedi Masters wait inside [S].
6. **Final room.** The Sith Master's remains drop a crystal, a double-bladed lightsaber and Sith gauntlets. Freedon Nadd's short lightsaber lies in the sarcophagus [S].
7. **Escape.** Copies of Nadd appear with high HP and fast regeneration. They vanish after one shot and move slowly [S].

### 2.7 Predatory flora and the dark side

- Some plants became predatory under Nadd's influence [S].
- Orbalisks feed on the dark side of the Force [S].
- The tomb's residual dark-side energy is the reason Nadd's worshippers and later Sith kept returning [S, and consistent with [L]].

## 3. Onderon and Iziz

### 3.1 The planet and the moon

- Onderon is a temperate Inner Rim world [S]. Its walled cities were built to keep Dxun's beasts out [S].
- The relationship is close and hostile. Onderon fears Dxun, and its exiles learned to ride Dxun's beasts. The moon is the planet's wild edge, and everything Iziz is built against.

### 3.2 Nadd, Sadow and the Beast Wars

- Freedon Nadd killed his master Naga Sadow and made himself tyrant of Iziz, reshaping its culture around Sith ideals [S, from a Star Wars: The Old Republic codex summary].
- Nadd banished criminals beyond the walls. Many survived and tamed beasts as mounts, becoming the Beast Riders [S].
- Around 4000 BBY the Beast Riders raided Iziz and kidnapped Princess Galia. Jedi who followed her reached the Kira fortress of Beast-Lord Modon Kira, and a tamed boma guided them there [S].
- Beast Riders later fought Exar Kun and the Mandalorians during the Great Sith War [S].
- Sources disagree on several dates and events. Label this material Legends in game text.

### 3.3 Iziz

- A walled city. The gate is a bridge guarded by turrets and a ray shield [S].
- Angular architecture: rectangular blocks, pyramidal temples such as the Unifar Temple, arched entrances and pillars [S].
- Light and dark brown walls, capped with blue roofs. The Unifar Temple uses blue and yellow [S].
- Sparse plant life: a few ferns on the façades [S].
- The Royal Palace stands on a hill above the centre. A stone-and-steel ramp climbs from the starport to the southern gates. The throne room was on the upper floors and was later moved to the rear. Ray shields and automated turrets protect it [S].

### 3.4 Queen Talia and General Vaklu (KOTOR II)

- Talia is Onderon's queen. Her cousin Vaklu is the army general who wanted Onderon free of the Republic. He secretly allied with the Sith [S].
- He declared martial law and deposed Talia. His assault on the palace failed and he was imprisoned. The final confrontation was in the throne room [S].
- One summary says Nihilus offered soldiers and firepower in exchange for access to Nadd's tomb, and Vaklu accepted [S, single source].
- Spelling varies between "Vaklu" and "Valku" in sources. The game uses "Vaklu".

### 3.5 Beast Riders as a culture

- Descended from Nadd's exiles [S]. Drexl mounts and boma steeds [S].
- [D] Gear: hide and bone tack, feathered standards and scarred mounts with tribal sigils. Camps in the canopy. They hate the Republic and the Sith equally.

## 4. Hidden base blueprint

### 4.1 Warren hub (Rathatri's temple)

Footprint about 110 m by 80 m, one surface ruin and a lower level. Bracketed references point to lorebook section 5.7 [L]. Dimensions are [D].

Route from the surface down:

1. **Approach trail (150 m).** A path that leads nowhere obvious, with cannok runs and zakkeg nests along the edges. Orbalisk caves open on the slope. Snare lines hang from trees. The trail is hinted at through roots and fallen columns.
2. **Snare clearing (40 x 40 m) [L: landing clearing].** The ship landing, hidden by canopy. A ring of mines and snares. Listening posts in the trees. Two scavenged turret pits. A repair gantry for the Harbinger. This is the first alarm point.
3. **Hidden entrance.** A cliff face of cracked stone with a slab door. It opens only after a ward-stone sequence of three timed prompts. A failed prompt triggers a snare field.
4. **Ward hall (12 x 30 m).** A corridor of cracked statues and carved murals with a grate chokepoint. First ward stone, which defenders can activate.
5. **Command room (14 m diameter) [L: lines 649 to 653].** A circular stone table with etched sigils. Traya's holocron sits on a plinth at the centre. Wall maps double as planet select. A passage on the north wall leads to the trophy gallery.
6. **Workshop (18 x 12 m) [L: lines 670 to 684].** Cracked stone tables, a magnifier bench under the only steady light, and caged subjects in a side room. The YARD gantry lives here.
7. **Assassin quarters (20 x 10 m) [L: line 641].** Twelve burial niches in two rows, 3 m deep. Seven slots for the Elite Five, plus Vaara and Eren.
8. **Rathatri's cell (6 x 6 m) [L: lines 654 to 657].** A clean floor etched with sigils, an altar with incense burners and a black candle, and a locked chest.
9. **Trophy gallery (30 m corridor) [L: line 653].** Hilts, cracked masks and claws. Populated by real kills.
10. **Lower level (unlocked in Act III onward) [L: line 643].** A forgotten Sith lab and a beast lair. Expansion for the Act IV dungeon.

Defence logic: the hidden entrance is the only sound way in. Defenders activate the ward stones. A HEAT 75 raid sends intruders down the approach to the clearing first.

### 4.2 Tomb of Freedon Nadd: six-chamber dungeon

This is the Act IV boss level. The prize is Nadd's holocron shard. The chambers are [D], built on [S] features.

1. **The Apron (landing pad).** A bare, cracked stone ring around the spire. Hazard: loose stone and open sky. Guardians: two orbalisks in alcoves in the apron wall. Entry is through the breach.
2. **Exar Kun's Breach (40 m tunnel).** The wound in the outer wall [S]. Hazard: sharp edges of Mandalorian iron and a collapsing lip that drops the player to chamber 3 if pushed. Guardians: an orbalisk swarm dropping from the ceiling.
3. **Mural Hall.** Carved murals of Nadd [D]. Hazard: sigil traps that drain the Force bar when stepped on. Guardian: a Sith spirit that applies a fear aura. Breaking the mural glyph removes it.
4. **Orbalisk Nest.** A dark cave under the spire, consistent with the orbalisk caves in [S]. Dormant orbalisks wake on noise. Hazard: smother cloud. Guardian: an orbalisk matriarch with a lightsaber-resistant shell. Weakness: the neck seam. Reward: orbalisk armour fragments.
5. **Trial Corridor.** Three trial rooms [D]. The KOTOR II tomb also has trials [S]. Pain: Force-drain pillars that tax the player's sustain. Doubt: copies of Nadd with high HP and fast regeneration that vanish after one shot [S]. Hunger: an orbalisk swarm on a narrow bridge.
6. **Nadd's Sanctum.** The sarcophagus and ritual circle. Guardians: a Sith Master and two Dark Jedi Masters, fought one at a time [S, from the KOTOR II final room]. Hazard: a ritual circle that burns when stood on. Prize: Nadd's short lightsaber and the holocron shard.

## 5. Visual bible

Colours are hex values. "Fog" means exponential-squared fog in three.js.

### 5.1 Dxun jungle

- **Palette:** canopy shade `#17301C`, mid leaf `#2F5A2C`, fern `#4A7A3A`, moss `#223A24`, mud `#3B2D20`, wet basalt `#1B1A18`, fog `#5E6F5C`, storm sky `#2A3038`, canopy light `#C8D8A0`.
- **Dark-side accents:** crimson `#9A1A2E` and violet `#4B1F5E`, used only for sigil and nexus glow.
- **Fog:** density 0.018 in clear weather, colour `#5E6F5C`. In a storm, density 0.03 and colour `#3A4450`.
- **Light:** key light from a low angle, colour `#C8D8A0` at intensity 0.8, with cone-shaped shafts at 4% opacity. Hemisphere light: sky `#5E7A5C`, ground `#1A140E`, intensity 0.6.
- **Mood:** humid, heavy and oppressive. A storm pulls the whole screen toward storm sky.
- **Props:** vines (tube geometry with noise), fern cards (instanced), root arches (torus slices), fallen logs, mud pools with a high-specular water surface `#2A3A40`.
- **Materials:** bark from ridged fBm stretched vertically. Leaves as alpha-cut cards with radial vein noise. Mud with bump noise.

### 5.2 Warren temple interior

- **Palette:** sandstone-basalt walls `#5B5148`, recesses `#2A2420`, moss stains `#3A4A2A`, sigil glow `#B8263A`, Traya holocron light `#3FA7E8`, glow rod `#E8D9A0` (flickering), scorch black `#151313`, burnt metal `#2B2622`, bronze patina `#6E8A6A`.
- **Light:** low ambient `#20201C`. Point lights in glow-rod tone with a flicker function (sine plus random dropouts). The holocron point light is cool blue and pulses at 0.5 Hz.
- **Props:** cracked statues (hooded figures with chipped faces), carved mural panels with rim light, circular stone table with emissive inlay lines, wall-map slates, trophy wall (hilts on wooden pegs), burial niches (arched recesses 1.6 m tall), altar with incense smoke particles, black candle, chalk circles as decals.
- **Materials:** rough stone from Worley cells for cracks, with fBm for the surface. Sigils drawn on canvas as stamp lines and used as an emissive map. Wet sheen in the sigil room by setting roughness to 0.35.

### 5.3 Iziz

- **Palette:** walls light brown `#9A7A55`, dark brown `#5E4632`, roofs blue `#2F5C9E`, Unifar blue `#2A5FA8`, Unifar yellow `#E0B43A`, palace stone `#B9A98A`, steel trim `#6E7278`, sparse ferns `#4C6B3A`.
- **Sky:** dusty daylight at the top `#D9C8A0`, horizon haze `#B8A27A`. Warm sun `#FFE3B0` from a high angle, with long shadows.
- **Fog:** none inside the city. Horizon haze only.
- **Geometry:** rectangular blocks, pyramids, arched gateways and pillars built from box and extrude primitives.
- **Props:** turrets (dome or hard point), ray shield (cyan `#7FD6E6` at 25% opacity), gate bridge, starport ramp, blue and yellow banners.

### 5.4 Tomb of Freedon Nadd

- **Palette:** obsidian `#0C0D10`, Mandalorian iron `#3A3F45`, iron highlights `#8C98A6`, edge chips `#C4CCD4`, rust-dark `#2A2420`, apron stone `#5D5F63`, Sith glow veins `#8B1A2F`, violet ambient `#3E1A4A`.
- **Light:** near-black tones, rim-lit from an overcast sky. Low sun from the left. Base fog `#4A5560`.
- **Geometry:** stepped trapezoid tiers from ExtrudeGeometry. Spiked bastion [D]: a ring of cones. Dry trench [D]: a negative-space ring with darker ambient occlusion.
- **Materials:** obsidian as high-gloss with conchoidal ridges from sine ripples on the normal map. Mandalorian iron as brushed anisotropic noise, a rivet grid and cracks. Murals as glyph stamps.

### 5.5 Procedural recipes

- Stone: fBm at 4 to 5 octaves, plus Worley for cracks.
- Obsidian: sine ripples along normals, roughness 0.1.
- Iron: streak noise along U, rivet grid every 0.5 m, cracks from Worley edges.
- Leaf: alpha cards with radial vein noise.
- Bark: ridged fBm stretched vertically.
- Primitives: Box, Cylinder, Cone, Torus, ExtrudeGeometry (trapezoids), TubeGeometry (vines), InstancedMesh (ferns, and cannok herds).
